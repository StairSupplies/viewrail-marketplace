# Viewrail Context for A3 Coaching

Loaded on demand to ground A3 conversations in Viewrail's strategic framework. Use this to help the leader connect their A3 to a Must Win, a True North gap, or a department goal.

## Just Cause and True North

- **Just Cause** , elevate the quality of life for our people, our customers, and our planet.
- **True North** , Perfect Projects: on-time, right first time. Most A3s at Viewrail trace to a True North gap.

## Embed Continuous Improvement

- The strategic goal that A3s most directly support, owned by Joe Cooper.
- **Must Wins under Embed CI:**
  - **MW1** , Reduce warranty / remake costs to less than 1.5% of revenue (Joe Cooper).
  - **MW7** , Project Design as the organizational constraint (Joe Cooper).
  - **MW8** , 95% of key-account projects on-time, right first time (Joe Cooper).
- An A3 framed as supporting one of these Must Wins has clear strategic alignment.

## Hoshin Kanri (strategy deployment)

- Bi-weekly cadence at Viewrail; Must Win owners submit pre-reads.
- A completed A3 is good evidence to attach to a Hoshin pre-read, particularly under Embed CI.

## VPS (Viewrail Performance System)

- VPS is the framework for how Viewrail develops people. Phase 1 is currently active and includes Performance Drivers, Career Ladders, EDPs, 1:1s, and Performance Reviews.
- A3 work isn't part of VPS directly, but A3 is how Viewrail does CI on the *systems* that VPS sits inside.

## When the A3 is about people performance

If the A3 is rooted in a behavioral or performance gap, that's a Performance Driver / 1:1 / EDP conversation, not (strictly) an A3. Coach the leader toward the right tool. A3 stays for system and process problems.
