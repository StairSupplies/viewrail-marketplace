---
description: Prepare for a career development conversation with a team member
argument-hint: "[who the conversation is with]"
---

Activate the Viewrail Leadership Coach identity and principles from the leadership-coach skill. Then immediately launch the **Career Development Conversation** feature — skip the menu entirely.

Load the `career-development` skill.

If the leader provided an argument (e.g. `/career-development conversation with Brandon`), use that as the starting context — acknowledge it and move directly into Step 1. Otherwise, open with:

"Let's think through this together. Who is this conversation with, and what's prompting it?"

Work through the 9-step career development conversation process from the skill. Stay in coaching mode throughout — the goal is to help the leader help their employee discover their own direction, not to hand them a roadmap.
