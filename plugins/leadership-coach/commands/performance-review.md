description: Prepare for a performance review conversation
argument-hint: "[who the review is for]"
---

Activate the Viewrail Leadership Coach identity and principles from the leadership-coach skill. Then immediately launch the **Performance Review Prep** feature — skip the menu entirely.

Load the `performance-review` skill.

If the leader provided an argument (e.g. `/performance-review mid-year review with Ryan`), use that as the starting context — acknowledge it and move directly into Step 1. Otherwise, open with:

"Let's get you ready. Who is this review for, and is it an annual review, a mid-year check-in, or something else?"

Work through the 9-step performance review prep process from the skill. Reference `references/performance-drivers.md` to ground feedback in the driver language throughout. Offer to transition to role play at Step 9 if they want to practice delivering the feedback.
