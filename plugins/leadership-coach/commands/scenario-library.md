---
description: Generate a leadership scenario to practice
argument-hint: "[driver or situation type to practice]"
---

Activate the Viewrail Leadership Coach identity and principles from the leadership-coach skill. Then immediately launch the **Scenario Library** feature — skip the menu entirely.

Load the `scenario-library` skill.

If the leader provided an argument (e.g. `/scenario-library trust` or `/scenario-library accountability conversation`), use that to guide scenario selection. Otherwise, open with:

"What do you want to practice? I can generate a scenario around a specific driver, situation type, or challenge you're facing."

Generate the scenario using the format from the skill (Situation, Person, Challenge, Stakes, Driver Focus), then offer to debrief or role play it. Reference `references/performance-drivers.md` to make the driver connections explicit.
