# Leadership vs. Management — Viewrail Leadership Reference

*Viewrail leadership development content — mapping the 6 drivers across both dimensions*

Great leaders integrate both leadership and management. This reference makes the distinction clear, using Viewrail's own Performance Drivers language. Use it when leaders confuse the two, over-index on one at the expense of the other, or need to see how the six drivers live across both dimensions.

---

## The Comparison

| Leadership | Management |
|------------|------------|
| **Focus:** People, vision, transformation | **Focus:** Systems, processes, execution |
| **Inspires and Connects** — rallies people around purpose and vision | **Plans and Organizes** — sets structure, resources, and timelines |
| **Develops People Through Coaching** — sparks self-discovery, builds ownership, grows confidence | **Empowers Through Ownership & Intent** — pushes decision-making closer to the work, surfaces gaps, and drives accountability |
| **Leads with an Outward Mindset** — sees others' needs and perspectives, fosters empathy and collaboration | **Drives Clarity & Purpose** — translates the vision into priorities, milestones, and measurable outcomes |
| **Builds Trust & Psychological Safety** — encourages open dialogue, inclusion, and risk-taking | **Ensures Accountability** — builds routines, monitors progress, delivers on commitments |
| **Practices Courageous Stewardship** — protects people and culture, makes values-based choices even under pressure | **Sustains Results** — ensures consistency, efficiency, and reliable execution |
| **Key Question:** *"Where are we going, and how do we bring out the best in people to get there?"* | **Key Question:** *"How do we structure, track, and sustain progress toward our goals?"* |

---

## The Balance

- **Leadership without management** = inspiring ideas but no follow-through.
- **Management without leadership** = efficient operations but no long-term meaning.
- **Effective leaders integrate both:** they inspire and coach people while also creating clarity and systems that deliver results.

---

## How to Use This in Coaching

When a leader is struggling, it often comes down to an imbalance in one of these dimensions. Use this reference to help them identify where they're over-indexing and where they have a development edge.

**Questions to surface the imbalance:**

- "Do you feel more comfortable in the leadership column or the management column? What does that tell you?"
- "Which of your direct reports needs more leadership from you right now? Which needs more management?"
- "Where are you spending your time this week — more in the left column or the right column? Is that what's needed?"

---

## Connection to All 6 Performance Drivers

This reference is a quick map of how all six drivers live within the leadership/management framework. When a leader sees their weakest driver, they can quickly locate whether it's a leadership gap (how they show up with people) or a management gap (how they create systems and structure).
