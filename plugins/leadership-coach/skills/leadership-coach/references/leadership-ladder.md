# The Leadership Ladder — Viewrail Leadership Reference

*Viewrail leadership development content — connecting leadership levels to the intent framework*

The Leadership Ladder describes five levels of employee ownership and thinking. Every coaching conversation is an opportunity to move someone one rung higher. This framework connects directly to *Turn the Ship Around!* (Driver 4: Empowers Through Ownership).

Use this reference when coaching leaders who are either doing too much for their team, or when preparing a coaching conversation designed to increase ownership and independent thinking.

---

## The Ladder of Leadership

Think of leadership as a ladder your team climbs toward ownership. Every conversation is a chance to move them one rung higher.

**Level 1: "Tell me what to do."**
They're waiting for direction. This is where most teams start — dependent and cautious.

**Level 2: "I think…"**
They begin to analyze situations but still rely on your judgment.
*Ask: "What leads you to think that?"*

**Level 3: "I recommend…"**
They're taking initiative and offering solutions.
*Ask: "What makes this the best option?"*

**Level 4: "I intend to…"**
They've thought it through and are ready to move forward.
*Ask: "What outcome do you expect?"*

**Level 5: "I've done…"**
They act with full ownership and accountability. You're leading a team of thinkers, not doers waiting for instruction.

---

## How Coaching Climbs the Ladder

Coaching isn't about having all the answers — it's about asking the right questions. When you coach instead of direct, you shift the mental load from you to them. Each time you hold back an answer and ask a question, you:

- **Develop capability** instead of dependency
- **Build trust** instead of control
- **Create clarity** instead of confusion

The result? A team that owns its work — and a leader who finally has time to think, plan, and lead at the next level.

---

## The Leadership Mindset Shift

| Old Model | Coaching Model |
|-----------|---------------|
| Leader provides answers | Leader builds thinkers |
| Team depends on authority | Team operates with clarity |
| Feedback happens after | Feedback happens through conversation |
| Progress is managed | Progress is owned |
| Leader is busy | Leader is free |

---

## Key Takeaway

Coaching isn't something extra you do. It's the system that gives you back your time, your focus, and your team's full potential. When your people think for themselves, you don't lose control — you gain capacity.

---

## Connection to Driver 4: Empowers Through Ownership

*Turn the Ship Around!* by Marquet gives us the "I intend to" language — pushing authority down so that the person closest to the work has both the clarity and the confidence to act. The Leadership Ladder is Viewrail's visual for that same shift: every rung up is a rung closer to "I intend to" and "I've done."

**Coaching move:** Ask a leader: "Where is each person on your team on this ladder right now? What would it take to move each of them one rung higher?"
